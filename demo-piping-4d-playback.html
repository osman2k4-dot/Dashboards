    How to read it. Every pipe spool carries a baseline erection week and, up to the data date, an actual erection week. Scrub or play through the weeks: green spools were erected on or ahead of plan, amber were erected late, red were due but are still not erected, and blue (after the data date) is what the baseline still expects. The S-curve says how much is behind; the model shows where it is and what is holding it.
