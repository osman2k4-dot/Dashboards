# Three-hour launch plan

Goal: by the end of the block, the template is purchasable, the portfolio is public, and ten people who could buy or hire have seen it.
Nothing here needs money up front.

## Hour 1 — Make it buyable (≈60 min)

1. **Turn on GitHub Pages** (5 min). Repo → Settings → Pages → Source: branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`. The portfolio goes live at `https://osman2k4-dot.github.io/Dashboards/`. Copy that link; it is used everywhere below.
2. **Open the template** in a browser, press "Download Excel template", and keep the .xlsx. Take three screenshots: full dashboard, the S-curve card, the Excel sheets. These are the product images.
3. **Create a Gumroad account** (free, takes 5 min). New product → Digital product. Paste from `gumroad-listing.md`. Upload a zip containing the HTML file, the .xlsx and a one-page README (copy the "How to use" text from the listing). Price: see listing. Publish.
4. **Add the Gumroad link** to the "Buy" button on `index.html` (search for `GUMROAD_URL`). Commit and push.

## Hour 2 — Get it in front of people (≈60 min)

5. **LinkedIn**: post the launch post from `linkedin-post.md` with the full-dashboard screenshot. Pin it to your profile. Update your headline to include "Builds single-file HTML dashboards for construction projects".
6. **Direct messages**: send the message in `cold-dm.md` to 10 planners, project engineers or site managers you know. Ask for feedback, not a sale. Two of ten usually reply, and that is where the first custom job comes from.
7. **Communities**: post the portfolio link (not the sales pitch) in one or two of: r/ProjectManagement, r/civilengineering, Planning Planet forum, the Primavera P6 LinkedIn groups. Lead with "built this for my site, open to feedback".

## Hour 3 — Open the services channel (≈45 min)

8. **Upwork or Fiverr**: create a gig from `freelance-gig.md`. Use the same screenshots. Services are where the first real money comes from; the template is the lead magnet and the proof.
9. **Set up one reusable "custom dashboard" offer** on Gumroad as well: "Your data, your branding, delivered in 5 days" at the price in the gig file. Buyers who want it done for them click that instead of the template.
10. **Schedule follow-up posts** for the next three weeks (the three posts in `linkedin-post.md`), one per week.

## What to expect

- Template sales alone: slow at first, a few per month once it ranks on Gumroad search. Do not judge it by week one.
- Services: the first "can you make one for my project" message is the real win. Price it from the gig tiers and reuse the template code, so a job is 3 to 6 hours of work.
- Every custom job adds a demo to the portfolio (with client data swapped for dummy data), which lifts the next one.
