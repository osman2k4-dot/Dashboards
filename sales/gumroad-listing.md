## Description

**Site progress your management will actually open.**

This is a single HTML file. Open it in any browser, drag your Excel sheet onto it, and you get:

- **Progress S-curve** — cumulative planned vs actual by week, with the data-date line and dashed forecast of the plan.
- **KPI tiles** — overall actual vs planned, schedule variance, SPI, this week's progress, forecast finish (remaining duration ÷ SPI) and manpower against plan.
- **Weekly progress bars** — incremental planned vs actual for the last 12 weeks.
- **Activity status table** — weighted activities with variance, colour-coded status (On track / Behind / Critical / Complete), sorted worst-first so the problems are on top.
- **Progress by area** — weighted completion per zone or package.
- **Manpower** — planned vs actual headcount by week.
- **Areas of concern** — your issues list, ranked by severity, with owners.

**Works the way sites actually work.**
Your data lives in a 5-sheet Excel workbook (Setup, Progress, Activities, Manpower, Concerns). The template is included and the dashboard can generate it for you. Fill it in on Friday, drop it onto the dashboard, print to PDF or email the HTML file. That is the weekly report done.

**No IT involved.** No installation, no server, no account, no subscription. Everything runs in the browser on your laptop. The data never leaves your machine. Works offline after the first load. Opens on a phone.
