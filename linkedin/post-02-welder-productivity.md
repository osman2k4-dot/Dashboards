## Main post (recommended)

Every EPC site tracks dia-inch. Almost none track it per welder.

On a piping job, the daily report says "142 DI today." Management nods. But that number hides the real story:

→ 6 welders carried 60% of the output.
→ 4 welders haven't crossed 3 DI/day all month.
→ Fit-up crews are running 2 days ahead of welding — joints are piling up, and nobody noticed.

So I built a dashboard that shows it. One HTML file, no Power BI license, no server.

What's inside:

▪ A 30-day matrix — every welder, every day, production DI per cell. Weak days glow, strong days stand out. One glance replaces an hour of Excel filtering.
▪ Fit-up (blue) vs welding (green), day by day, split green field / brown field — the gap between the two lines is your future backlog.
▪ Welder rankings and drill-down detail — who's performing, who needs support, who's on the wrong joint mix.
▪ Spools & joints and pipe-support tracking straight from the client DPR.

The interesting part isn't the charts. It's what happens the week after the dashboard hits the site:

Supervisors start asking about their own numbers before the meeting does. The fit-up/welding gap becomes a planning topic instead of a surprise. And "142 DI today" turns into a conversation about the 4 welders who need a different assignment.

Numbers in the demo are fictional (the "Orion Project"), but the structure and logic are identical to what runs on a live oil & gas EPC site every day.

Demo link in the comments — it opens in any browser, even on a phone.

If your productivity reporting is still a weekly Excel + PowerPoint ritual, I'd genuinely like to hear how you track welder-level performance. 👇
