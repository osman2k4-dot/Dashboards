A progress report tells you piping is 42 % welded. It does not tell you which 58 % to attack first.

I built a 4D progress model that joins the 3D model, the site spool and joint lists, the test-pack log and the P6 schedule, spool by spool. Watch 50 seconds (demo on fictional data).

What it shows:
• Every spool in its real position, coloured by site status: not received, received, doubled at ground, erected, fit-up done, welded.
• Pick a unit and the balance is visible at a glance: what is still on the ground, what has not even reached site.
• MC-01 loop view: the client's priority test packs drawn strong, everything else pale. That is the degreasing scope, in 3D.
• Test packs ranked by how much is already welded, so the crews go where the least work releases a hydrotest.
• One click lights a test pack in 3D: which spools to erect and weld next, and where they are.
• A plan summary per unit: rate needed against rate achieved, float to MC-01, and the three points where a slip moves the end date first.
• Press Play and the P6 plan runs forward to MC-01, MC-02 and completion.

The numbers were always in the reports. Seeing them on the plant changes the meeting from "how much is behind" to "what do we finish first". A construction manager, a client rep or someone who never opens P6 reads it in seconds.

On my live project the same model runs on the real 3D model, site lists and P6 schedule. One HTML file that opens in any browser. Built with Claude Code.
