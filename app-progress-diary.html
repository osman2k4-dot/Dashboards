Step 1

Area planners enter progress

Each planner picks their area and enters quantities, manpower, equipment and notes for the day.

Step 2 · Excel

Workbook updates itself

Every entry is written as a row in the Progress Log, Manpower Log, Equipment Log and Site Notes sheets of the linked .xlsx file.

Step 3 · Excel

Formulas roll it up

The Summary sheet uses SUMIFS on the logs, so cumulative quantities and % complete recalculate in Excel for any date typed in cell B1.

Step 4

Daily diary is produced

The Daily Diary sheet and the diary page here are generated from the same log. Nobody writes the diary by hand.
